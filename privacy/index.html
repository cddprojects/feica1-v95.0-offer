Privacy Policy

Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) — Canada

Talent Fluxo is committed to protecting your personal information in line with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). If you live in Alberta, British Columbia, or Quebec, provincial privacy law may also apply. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

1. Who We Are

Talent Fluxo helps people in Canada find part-time work from home. References to "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to Talent Fluxo and its authorized staff.

For privacy questions, contact us at: hello@talentfluxo.com

2. What Personal Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, we collect the following:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Cell number

We do not collect Social Insurance Numbers, financial account details, or medical information through this website.

3. Why We Collect Your Information

Your information is collected for the following purposes:

  • To see if the part-time work is a fit
  • To contact you by WhatsApp or email about your request
  • To add you as a Talent Fluxo member if you are selected
  • To send relevant updates about opportunities (you may opt out at any time)

We will not use your information for any purpose not listed above without your consent.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used by Talent Fluxo staff only. We use it to:

  • Review your profile and determine suitability
  • Reach out to you via WhatsApp within 24 hours if you are a suitable candidate
  • Coordinate work assignments and payment if you join as a member

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared with:

  • Internal Talent Fluxo team members involved in recruitment and operations
  • Service providers who assist us in operating our systems (e.g. cloud storage, communication tools) — bound by confidentiality obligations

We will only disclose your information to external parties if required by Canadian law or a Canadian authority.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

  • If you don't join: We keep your information for up to 12 months from the day you contact us, then delete it.
  • Active members: We keep your information for as long as you work with us, and for up to 2 years after that.

7. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or changes. These include access controls, secure communication channels, and staff confidentiality obligations.

8. Your Rights Under PIPEDA

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information
  • Withdraw consent for us to use your information at any time (this may affect our ability to process your application)
  • Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal requirement to keep it

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@talentfluxo.com. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. Basic browser functionality may use session cookies which are not used to identify you personally.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be reflected with an updated date below. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

For any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information:

  • Email: hello@talentfluxo.com
  • Organization: Talent Fluxo (for people working in Canada)

You may also file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada if you believe your privacy rights have been infringed.
